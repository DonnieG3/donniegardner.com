Donnie Gardner

Poetry

Erosion

October 31, 2026

Youth is waisted when we have it,
regret once it’s wasted.

Your indifference is a cancer, consuming me.
My regrets are a cancer,
consuming us.

What is left… the waist.

Life consumed like unused expired calendars, empty and left in a junk drawer.
The excrement of wasted time, discarded minutes misused as if infinite when they were precious diamonds,
a finite commodity.
