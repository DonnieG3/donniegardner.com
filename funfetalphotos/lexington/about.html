About the Owner

Welcome to Fun Fetal Photos! I’m so glad you’re here.

I’m a wife, mom, entrepreneur, and registered ultrasound technologist with 12 years of experience. Earlier in my career, I had the privilege of working at Fun Fetal Photos for six years, where I helped countless families experience the excitement of seeing their little one before they arrived.

Many people in our community also know me as the owner of Fizz & Cream, where I’ve enjoyed serving families at schools, sporting events, festivals, and community events. Creating memorable experiences and building relationships has always been my passion, and that’s something I carry with me in everything I do.

I am incredibly honored to be continuing the legacy of Fun Fetal that so many families have come to know and love. Having previously been part of the Fun Fetal family, this opportunity feels especially meaningful. It truly feels like coming home, and I’m excited to continue providing the caring, welcoming experience that has made Fun Fetal Photos so special over the years.

There is nothing more rewarding than watching parents, siblings, grandparents, and loved ones fall in love with their baby before they’re even born. Every little yawn, stretch, smile, and wave is a reminder of how amazing this journey is, and I never take for granted the opportunity to be part of such a special moment.

Whether you’re visiting for your very first ultrasound or coming back to watch your baby grow, my goal is to make every appointment relaxing, memorable, and filled with joy. I want every family who walks through our doors to feel welcomed, celebrated, and cared for.

Thank you for trusting me to be part of your pregnancy journey. I look forward to meeting you, celebrating with your family, and continuing the tradition of making unforgettable memories at Fun Fetal Photos for years to come.




    FAQ's

  • When can I find out the gender?
  • We can do gender scans starting at 16 weeks.
  • When can I get cute face pictures?
  • Prime time for chunky faces is 28-32 weeks Twins 26-29 weeks.
  • When is the earliest I can get a scan?
  • We start scanning at 10 weeks.
  • Will I always get cute picture?
  • We try very hard to get the best from every baby. Every pregnancy and baby are different and we will strive for the best.
  • What can I do to improve my scan?
  • Drink LOTS of Water and follow all instructions given.
  • Do you charge more for twins?
  • No, twins are never charged more but we do need to know when scheduling twins up front as they take more time.
  • Do you schedule online?
  • No please call to schedule we like talking personally with all our families to make sure they understand everything and to better answer question.
  • Can I bring friends and family?
  • Well of course you can. We are family and kid friendly.
  • Can you keep gender secret?
  • Sure if you don't want to know or want it sealed in a envelope let the Tech know at the beginning of the scan.
  • Can you tell me what my baby weighs?
  • Sorry we can not do weight as that is considered diagnostic.
  • Do you have Heartbeat bears?
  • Yes we have a whole line of Heartbeat Animals and they are $30.00 & 35.00 depending on style.
  • What is a 3D Ultrasound?
  • A 3D ultrasound refers to the rendering of ultrasound data into three dimensional volumes to easily view your baby, it can also be used in other types of medican scans. These scans can be referred to as a 4D Ultrasounds when it involves watching the utrasound in live motion over time.
