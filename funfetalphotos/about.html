Meet the owner

Welcome to Fun Fetal Photos! I’m so glad you’re here.

I’m a wife, mom, entrepreneur, and registered ultrasound technologist with 12 years of experience. Earlier in my career, I had the privilege of working at Fun Fetal Photos for six years, helping countless families experience the excitement of seeing their little one before they arrived.

Many people in our community also know me as the owner of Fizz & Cream. Creating memorable experiences and building relationships has always been my passion, and that is something I carry with me in everything I do.

I am incredibly honored to continue the Fun Fetal legacy that so many families know and love. Having previously been part of the Fun Fetal family, this opportunity feels especially meaningful. It truly feels like coming home.

There is nothing more rewarding than watching parents, siblings, grandparents, and loved ones fall in love with their baby before they’re even born. My goal is to make every appointment relaxing, memorable, and filled with joy.

I can’t wait to celebrate with your family.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Every baby has their own schedule. Here are a few things that can help you plan the perfect visit.

Call to schedule
When can I find out the gender?

Gender scans are available starting at 16 weeks.

When is the best time for face pictures?

Prime time for sweet, chunky faces is 28–32 weeks, or 26–29 weeks for twins.

When is the earliest I can get a scan?

We begin scanning at 10 weeks.

How can I improve my scan?

Drink lots of water and follow all preparation instructions provided before your appointment.

Do you charge more for twins?

No. Twins are never charged more, but please let us know when scheduling because those appointments need extra time.

Can I bring friends and family?

Of course. We are family- and kid-friendly, with a large viewing room for the people you love.

Can you keep the gender a secret?

Absolutely. Tell your technologist at the beginning of the scan and we can seal the news in an envelope.

Do you have heartbeat animals?

Yes. We offer a selection of heartbeat animals priced at $30 or $35 depending on the style.

What is a 3D or 4D ultrasound?

A 3D ultrasound renders ultrasound data into three-dimensional images so you can see your baby more clearly. A 4D ultrasound adds live movement over time.
